# Curriculum RL on Entry Timing

Agent trained progressively: late-window entries first (easy),
then extending to earlier decisions (harder).

# Curriculum RL Results

- **late_only**: best acc 46.4%, best EV/trade -0.0552, mean acc 46.4%
- **late_quarter**: best acc 46.4%, best EV/trade -0.0552, mean acc 46.4%
- **half_window**: best acc 46.4%, best EV/trade -0.0552, mean acc 46.4%
- **full_window**: best acc 46.4%, best EV/trade -0.0552, mean acc 46.4%
